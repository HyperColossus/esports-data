# esports-data

Public data feed for the esports prediction pages at https://hypercoloss.us/esports.html.

Files under `v1/` are generated by the prediction bot (HyperColossus/Esports-Prediction-Bot) and replaced on every publish. Do not edit them by hand.

Contents: posted forecasts with verified results and corrections, and the bot's paper bets in units and percentages. No stakes, account details or controls are published. Contract: `docs/operations/public-website-contract.md` in the bot repository.
